Privacy policy

Graphline watch face · Last updated 8 November 2026

Graphline is a watch face for Garmin watches. This page explains what information Graphline uses and what happens to it.

In short

Graphline does not collect, send or sell any information about you. Everything it shows is read and drawn on your watch, and nothing leaves it.

What Graphline uses on your watch

  • Heart rate and stress history. Graphline reads these from your watch to draw its graphs and show your heart rate.
  • Your last known location. Graphline uses it to work out sunrise and sunset. It keeps this location on your watch only.
  • Activity data, such as steps, calories, floors climbed and active minutes, if you choose to show them.
  • Your settings and saved setups. These are kept on your watch, and Garmin keeps your settings in the Connect IQ app so you can change them on your phone.

What Graphline does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet.
  • It does not send any information to the developer or to anyone else.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking or ads.

Weather

The weather shown on Graphline comes from Garmin, through your phone and the Garmin Connect app. Graphline only displays it.

Buying Graphline

Payments are handled by Garmin in the Connect IQ Store. The developer does not receive your payment details. Garmin's own privacy policy applies to the store, your Garmin account and your payment.

Deleting your information

To remove everything Graphline keeps, remove Graphline from your watch. This deletes its settings, saved setups and the stored location.

Changes to this policy

If Graphline ever starts using information in a new way, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.
