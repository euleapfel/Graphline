Graphline

A watch face for Garmin with a big clock, four numbers you choose and a graph of your day.

Change the settings

On your watch: press and hold the watch face, choose Graphline, then Settings.

On your phone: open the Connect IQ app, go to My Device, Watch Faces, Graphline, Settings.

No forecast?

The weather comes from your phone. Keep your phone connected to your watch and open the Garmin Connect app once, so the watch gets the latest forecast.
